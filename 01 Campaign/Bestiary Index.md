## Everything else: 2014 stat blocks
Most monsters in the book refer to the **2014 Monster Manual**. The *Fantasy Statblocks* plugin comes with the free 5e SRD (2014) bestiary, so this code block:

````
```statblock
monster: Hill Giant
```
````

renders a full hill giant stat block right inside a note.

> [!warning] Don't mix in the 2024 Monster Manual
> Your table plays 2014 rules as written. 2024 stat blocks for the same monsters have different numbers and actions.
> Only use the 2024 book for monsters that don't exist in 2014 (which also makes them homebrew for your table).
