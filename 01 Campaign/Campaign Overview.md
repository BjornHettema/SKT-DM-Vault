# Campaign Overview

**Adventure:** *Storm King's Thunder*, levels 1 to 11 · **Rules:** 2014 5e, rules as written, no homebrew · **Style:** sandbox, very long campaign (10+ sessions / 30+ hours)
**Table:** Tabletop Simulator for maps and minis, Discord for voice, text and dice.

## The big picture (DM only)
Read [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=8|Adventure Background, p. 7]] and the [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=18|adventure flowchart, p. 17]].

## Chapters
| Chapter | Starts | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| [[Intro - Introduction]] | [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=8\|p. 7]] | Why the North is shaking: the giants' ordning has been shattered by the gods, the storm giant king Hekaton has vanished, and every giant clan is scrambling to prove itself worthy of the top spot. Read this before anything else. |
| [[Ch 01 - A Great Upheaval]] | [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=20\|p. 19]] | Starter chapter that takes 1st-level characters to 5th. A giant raid has left the village of Nightstone in ruins; the party deals with the aftermath, the goblins in the Dripping Caves, and a flying wizard's tower. |
| [[Ch 02 - Rumblings]] | [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=38\|p. 37]] | The party arrives in one of three towns (Bryn Shander, Goldenfields or Triboar) just as giants attack. Each town has 'special NPCs' who give individual characters a personal quest and a faction tie. |
| [[Ch 03 - The Savage Frontier]] | [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=64\|p. 63]] | The sandbox chapter: peoples of the North, random wilderness encounters, and a gazetteer of places. Players travel toward the goals set in chapter 2 until they meet Harshnag. |
| [[Ch 04 - The Chosen Path]] | [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=122\|p. 121]] | Harshnag guides the party to the Eye of the All-Father, where an oracle tells them how to find King Hekaton. Along the way: a cult airship and a first brush with the true villain. |
| [[Ch 05 - Den of the Hill Giants]] | [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=138\|p. 137]] | Chief Guh's stronghold, Grudd Haug. One of five giant-lord chapters; usually the party visits only the one the oracle points to. |
| [[Ch 06 - Canyon of the Stone Giants]] | [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=146\|p. 145]] | Thane Kayalithica's canyon home, Deadstone Cleft. Giant-lord chapter. |
| [[Ch 07 - Berg of the Frost Giants]] | [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=156\|p. 155]] | Jarl Storvald's iceberg fortress Svardborg and his ship the Krigvind. Giant-lord chapter. |
| [[Ch 08 - Forge of the Fire Giants]] | [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=168\|p. 167]] | Duke Zalto's volcanic forge, Ironslag. Giant-lord chapter. |
| [[Ch 09 - Castle of the Cloud Giants]] | [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=188\|p. 187]] | Countess Sansuri's flying castle, Lyn Armaal. Giant-lord chapter. |
| [[Ch 10 - Hold of the Storm Giants]] | [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=202\|p. 201]] | Maelstrom, the undersea court of the storm giants, where Hekaton's daughters hold the throne and the party learns what happened to the king. |
| [[Ch 11 - Caught in the Tentacles]] | [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=216\|p. 215]] | The hunt for the captive king across the Trackless Sea, involving the Golden Goose, the Grand Dame and the Morkoth. |
| [[Ch 12 - Doom of the Desert]] | [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=226\|p. 225]] | The finale: tracking the true villain to a lair in the desert and ending the threat with storm giant allies. |
