# Table Rules (Session 0)

These are the rulings you agreed with the players at session 0. A player-facing copy is in the Player Wiki as `Table Charter`.
**Whenever you change a rule, change both copies.**

> [!note] DM reminders drawn from these rules
> - **Inspiration**: rules as written, you decide when to give it, and the players asked you to *remind yourself*. There is a tracker in every session note.
> - **Alignment is strictly enforced.** Each PC note has an alignment field. Log alignment-relevant choices in the session note.
> - **Only 1 absence per player, with your approval.** Attendance is tracked in each session note's `attendees` field.
> - **Private messages:** DM-to-PC is allowed, PC-to-PC is strongly discouraged, and sharing secrets that way is not allowed. Give each PC a private Discord channel with you.
> - **Unannounced dice rolls are not valid.** In Discord, rolls go in #dice-rolls (or through Avrae). In TTS, roll only after you call for it.
> - **Traps:** use the trap rules in *Xanathar's Guide to Everything*.
> - **Death is permanent.** New characters are allowed. Keep a spare 1st-level hook handy for each chapter.
