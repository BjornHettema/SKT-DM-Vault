# Guide to the Guide - Takeaways

Notes from [[Guide to the guide.pdf|A Guide to Storm King's Thunder]] (community guide, 64 pages), focused on what's useful for our table: a new DM, new players, 2014 rules as written.

## For running chapter 1 (Nightstone)
- Chapter 1 is straightforward and should take **a few sessions**. Use it to find your feet.
- **Warn the players** that some encounters are far too strong for them, and that running away or talking is a valid choice. *(Done: it's in the welcome guide and in [[Session 001 - Nightstone]].)*
- **When a fight is going badly,** the book suggests capturing characters instead of killing them. Our table rule is that death is permanent, but capture is a fair way out of a fight that was clearly too big.
- **Warm-up fight idea:** Kella, posing as a monk, being attacked by four goblins outside the village. It's an easy first fight and a way to meet her. We use the campfire start instead; keep this in reserve.
- **Rillix the tressym** (winged cat, Xelbrin house): a player may want it as a pet. Let them. The Goldenfields quest later asks the party to bring it to Miros Xelbrin.

## Before session 2 or 3
- [ ] **Magic item wish lists.** The book rolls on the DMG magic item tables constantly. Ask each player to pick about 3 items they'd love from each table, then roll from their lists instead. (Book: random magic items [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=19|p. 18]].)
- [ ] **Pre-roll the giant loot.** Do the treasure rolls before the session; rolling at the table slows it down.
- [ ] **Foreshadowing to start planting early:**
    - giant **runes** on the giants' belts, which pay off at the Eye of the All-Father in chapter 4;
    - legends of **Iymrith**, the blue dragon;
    - stories of **Harshnag** and his old adventuring band, the Gray Hands;
    - rumours of a **kraken cult**;
    - the red dragon **Klauth**, who matters in chapter 4.

## Chapter 2: pick one town
- Pick **one** town instead of offering all three. The book's three quests from [[Morak Ur'gray]] are how you steer the party (book [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=33|p. 32]]).
- **Recommended for this party: [[Bryn Shander]].** Sir Baric's quest there leads to Neverwinter and then on to **Helm's Hold**, the extremist Helm monastery ([[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=45|p. 44]], [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=91|p. 90]]). That gives [[Wolfram Erenwald]]'s memory story a natural path.
- **Alternative: [[Goldenfields]].** It has a Zhentarim spy ([[Shalvus Martholio]]) for [[B'Leep]], a cleric NPC for [[Sylvaris]], and the tressym quest.
- **How chapter 2 works:** during the giant attack, each player also controls one townsperson. Surviving townspeople give the party quests, and those quests become the party's first sandbox leads. The author suggests letting the party meet those townspeople *before* the attack so it doesn't feel forced.

## Chapter 3 and later
- Distances are huge (Bryn Shander to Waterdeep is about 600 miles). Summarise long journeys in a few sentences so sessions don't turn into filler.
- **The hidden story (DM only):** Hekaton's elder daughters **Mirran and Nym** hired the Kraken Society to kill their mother, Queen Neri. **Iymrith** is manipulating them. **Serissa**, the youngest, is the daughter the party should end up supporting. Hekaton and his brother Uthor distrust small folk, and part of the arc is changing their minds.
- **Optional recurring ally:** Felgolos, a bronze dragon. The author introduces him early so the players care later when he turns up captured in the cloud giant castle (chapter 9).
