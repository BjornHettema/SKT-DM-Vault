# Xolkin Alassandar

**One-liner:** Half-elf leader of the **Seven Snakes**, Zhentarim mercenaries who do dirty work for "the Snail" in Daggerford. Charming and ruthless.
**Book:** [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=28|p. 27-28]]
**Wants:** to help [[Kella Darkhope]] take Nightstone for the Black Network, and Kella herself. He loves her; she doesn't love him back.
**Voice / mannerism:** smooth, flattering, smiles too much; calls everyone "friend".
**Stat block:** Bandit Captain (2014), with six Bandits.
