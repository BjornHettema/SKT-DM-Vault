# Xolkin Alassandar

**One-liner:** Half-elf leader of the **Seven Snakes**, Zhentarim mercenaries who do dirty work for "the Snail" in Daggerford. Charming and ruthless.
**Book:** [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=28|p. 27-28]]
**Wants:** to help [[Kella Darkhope]] take Nightstone for the Black Network, and Kella herself. He loves her; she doesn't love him back.
**Voice / mannerism:** smooth, flattering, smiles too much; calls everyone "friend".
**Stat block:** Bandit Captain (2014), with six Bandits.

## What the party knows
- Arrived at dawn with six riders (the Seven Snakes) to free Kella; the party recognised them as Zhentarim (Session 2).
- Let into the village. Deal: they help fight the orcs, then leave and pay five gems ("emeralds"). **He doesn't intend to leave** (DM).

## DM-only secrets
- Will trade five 100 gp gems for Kella's life, and use force if that fails.
- Drinks his *potion of invulnerability* when a fight starts.
- Owns the flying messenger snake. If it's dead, he sends a rider to Daggerford instead.
