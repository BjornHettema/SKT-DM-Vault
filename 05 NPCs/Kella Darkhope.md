# Kella Darkhope

**One-liner:** Zhentarim spy posing as a travelling monk, hiding in the wrecked Nightstone Inn.
**Book:** [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=26|p. 25]] (area 8f), Seven Snakes [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=28|p. 27]]
**Wants:** to turn Nightstone into a Zhentarim base. The giant attack emptied the village for her, and her reinforcements are on the way.
**Voice / mannerism:** quiet, calm and polite; answers a question with a question; strokes the snake on her arm.
**Stat block:** Spy (2014).

## What the party knows
- Found at the inn at 03:00, after the party's long rest: a quiet survivor who was left behind (Session 1).
- B'Leep showed her his Zhentarim tattoo. She asked to speak with him alone; the rest of the party agreed. They half-trust her (Session 1).
- **Pending:** B'Leep's private talk with her, played between sessions 1 and 2. Record the outcome here.

## DM-only secrets
- Had nothing to do with the giants; she only takes advantage of the attack.
- Shot the goblin in the inn's dining room minutes before the party arrives.
- The flying snake belongs to [[Xolkin Alassandar]] and brought the message "On our way."
- Cover story if found: a guest knocked out by debris and left behind, then trapped by the goblins (Insight vs Deception).
- Will try to win over any party member with Zhentarim ties: [[B'Leep]].
