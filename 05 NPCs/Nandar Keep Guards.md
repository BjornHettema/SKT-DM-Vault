# Nandar Keep Guards

**One-liner:** Four guards (Sydiri Haunlar, Torem Breck, Alara Winterspell, Kaelen Zam) stranded in the half-collapsed keep with **Lady Nandar's body**. Shocked, demoralised and arguing; none of them is a leader.
**Book:** [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=27|p. 26]] (area 14a)
**Wants:** someone to take charge. They turn to the party for guidance.

**Give each a different opinion so the argument is easy to play** *(improv, not from the book)*:
| Guard | Wants to... | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| Sydiri Haunlar | Wait for orders from Waterdeep | By-the-book, keeps saying "protocol" |
| Torem Breck | Abandon Nightstone and run | Jittery, talks fast |
| Alara Winterspell | Go after the villagers in the caves | Angry, fists clenched |
| Kaelen Zam | Nothing - he's grieving Lady Nandar | Quiet, stares at her body |

## What they can tell the party
- A castle in the sky dropped rocks; the falling rocks cut the bridge to the village.
- The villagers lowered the drawbridge and fled north, to bat caves about a mile away. Some village guards went with them.
- Four pale giants came down, uprooted the nightstone and flew off east.
- Lady Nandar died under the rubble in the great hall.
