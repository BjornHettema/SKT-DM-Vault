## DM-only: hooks into Storm King's Thunder
*My suggestions - keep what you like, delete the rest.*
- She SPEAKS GIANT. In this campaign that makes her the party's diplomat and interpreter. Reward it: Harshnag, Zephyros, the oracle at the Eye, and Serissa's court all open up for someone who talks to giants rather than fights them.
- Her peacemaker personality versus a party that allows murder-hoboing and PvP: that tension is where the good dilemmas come from.
- The song fragment could be a giant lullaby, a Selûnite hymn, or half of a key. Decide what it is before chapter 4.
- The priest who raised her is a ready-made hostage or messenger for a chapter 2 or 3 hook.

## Backstory (as written by the player)
She was born to a human mother and an elven father. When she was twelve, her parents died, leaving her alone. A kind priest took her in and raised her in the temple, teaching her the importance of faith, kindness, and helping others.

As she grew older, she became a cleric and discovered a talent for bringing people together, even those who were enemies. She believes that almost every conflict can be solved through understanding and compassion.

At 28, she left the temple to travel the world and help those in need. She trusts deeply in her faith and believes that if she works hard and follows her beliefs, things will turn out well.
