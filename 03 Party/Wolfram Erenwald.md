## Backstory (as written by the player)
Wolfram Erenwald remembers being a soldier. He remembers the weight of armor, long marches, cold watches, shouted orders and the chaos of battle. He remembers enough to know that he served as a low-ranking infantryman and eventually held the rank of corporal. Much of who he is was forged during those years.

War taught Wolfram that when duty fails, the innocent often pay the price. He saw courage and cowardice, discipline and chaos, sacrifice and selfishness. From this he formed a simple conviction: those with the strength to protect others have an obligation to stand between them and danger.

What happened at the end of his service, however, Wolfram cannot remember. There is a void where those memories should be. His next clear memory is awakening wounded inside a temple of Helm. He does not know how he came to be there, who brought him there, or what nearly killed him.

Wolfram believes Helm saved him.

He does not claim to know how or why. To him, the important thing is that his life should have ended, yet he awoke beneath the protection of the Watcher. Wolfram interpreted his survival not as a reward, but as a command: his watch was not yet over.

He now carries the symbol of Helm wherever possible and offers his prayers silently. Armed with shield and warhammer, Wolfram has returned to the world as a paladin, determined to protect the innocent, confront those he considers enemies of the divine order, and never willingly abandon his post again.

Whatever happened before he awoke in Helm's temple remains unknown - even to Wolfram.
