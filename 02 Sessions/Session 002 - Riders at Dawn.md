## 2. The Seven Snakes (about 1 h)

**When:** right after the second one-on-one, *like clockwork*. Dawn is just breaking.

**Read aloud (your own words):**
> *The sky over the hills is turning grey. On the trail to the south: hoofbeats, many horses, coming fast. Seven riders in dark cloaks pull up at the edge of the moat. The one in front, a smiling half-elf with a gold ring, raises a gloved hand.*

**What he says first depends on the drawbridge:**
- **Drawbridge down:** they ride straight into the square and he calls out: *"Kella! Kella Darkhope!"*
- **Drawbridge up:** he calls up to the tower: *"Good morning, friends! Be so kind as to lower that bridge? We've ridden all night."*
- **The bounty-hunter story (book):** they planned to pose as bounty hunters chasing a wanted criminal, Kella. With guards on the towers, **Xolkin tries that story first**: *"We're hunting a thief. A woman named Kella Darkhope. There's good gold in it for anyone who helps."* He drops the act as soon as he sees the village is nearly empty, or the moment Kella shows herself.

**Xolkin Alassandar** ([[Xolkin Alassandar]]): half-elf Bandit Captain. Charming, ruthless, smiles too much, calls everyone "friend". **In love with Kella**; she doesn't love him back.

### What Kella does
- She tries to **let them in** (book). But the drawbridge needs **both winches at once**: if it's up, she needs a second person. That's where **B'Leep** comes in, if he agreed to help.
- If the party has been friendly, she **reveals she's Zhentarim** once the riders are close enough to protect her.
- If Wolfram read the letter, he already suspects her. Let that tension play out.

### How it can go

| The party... | The Zhents... | Book |
|---|---|---|
| **Lets them in / does nothing** | Raise the drawbridge behind them; Xolkin sends the flying snake to the Snail in Daggerford ("the village is ours"). If the snake is dead, a rider leaves for Daggerford. They **ignore the keep** (hard to reach), but urge friendly PCs to "deal with" the guards in it. | p. 28 |
| **Keeps the bridge up** | Can't get in: the palisade can't be climbed without gear. Xolkin talks, flatters, then threatens. Standoff. | p. 20, 27 |
| **Holds Kella prisoner** | Xolkin offers **five 100 gp gems** for her. If refused, he tries to free her by force. | p. 28 |
| **Fights** | Xolkin drinks his **potion of invulnerability** (resistance to all damage, 1 minute) and leaps in. Six bandits with light crossbows. **Far beyond deadly at level 1-2** in the open; only survivable from the towers with the bridge up. | p. 28 |
| **Comes to terms** | A deal: shared village, payment, safe passage. The Zhents will **help against the orcs** later ("Strange Bedfellows"). | p. 28 |

**The guards' view:** friendly with the party, they ask them to **save the village from the Zhentarim**. That's a real dilemma for B'Leep (and Wolfram, who just took command).

**Dilemmas to lean on:**
- B'Leep: Kella's gold vs. his friends, and vs. villagers who are prisoners.
- Wolfram: the Zhents want the guards gone; he just promised to protect them.
- Sylvaris (peacemaker): is there a deal that avoids bloodshed?

**Rewards if the Zhents are beaten or robbed:** Xolkin: gold ring with a black winged serpent (25 gp), 4 pp, 13 gp, five 100 gp gems, potion of invulnerability. Each bandit: 2d10 gp. **Seven riding horses.**

**Afterwards:** whatever happens, let them **take a long rest** if they want (book). **Level 2** if Gum-Gum was dealt with.

## 4. The Ear Seekers (about 1 h 45)

**When:** after the rest, in daylight, the next day. Foreshadow during the rest: horns and smoke over the forest to the north.

**Who:** war chief **Gurrash** (already wounded: **60 of 93 HP**), his lieutenant **Norgra One-Eye** (an Eye of Gruumsh, uninjured, takes command if Gurrash falls) and **20 orcs**. They wear **necklaces of elf ears**. They lost a battle with the wood elves of the Ardeep Forest and are fleeing south with the elves close behind. They want to make a stand in Nightstone. Details and numbers: [[Nightstone - Ear Seekers]].

**Read aloud:**
> *Shouting from the tree line to the north. Then they come: twenty-odd orcs, running hard, grey skin streaked with blood, necklaces of shrivelled elf ears swinging on their chests. At their head, a huge orc with a greataxe, arrows still sticking out of his shoulder, bellows at the others and points at your walls.*

**Shay (Orc):** he understands Gurrash's shouts: *"The walls! Take the walls! The pointy-ears are behind us!"* Later: *"Find another way in! Downriver!"* That's the warning the party needs.

### How the siege plays out (book p. 28)
1. **Drawbridge down:** they charge it and storm the village.
2. **Drawbridge up:** they swim the moat and try to climb the palisade. **They fail.** Gurrash sends scouts downriver.
3. **After 10 minutes** they find the way in: **the gap in the palisade where the broken bridge (11) runs up to the keep.** That's the weak point. Hold it, and only one or two orcs come through at a time.
4. **Inside the village** they fight to the death and take no prisoners.
5. **Hiding works:** the orcs don't know what they're up against. The party can hide in the village and pick off a few at a time.
6. **The keep is safe:** the orcs ignore it.
7. **Morale:** if they lose **more than half** (11+) without getting a foothold in the village, the survivors flee into the hills.

**If the Zhents are allies:** they raise the drawbridge, man the towers and shoot. They avoid melee and don't risk their necks.

**Elves to the rescue (your safety net):** if the party is about to be overwhelmed (two PCs down, or orcs pouring through the gap), **eight elf scouts** arrive from the north, led by **Rond Arrowhome**, daring and sharp-tongued. Afterwards he shouts *"You're welcome!"* and leads them back to the forest. They want no reward, and **no peace talks** with Nightstone. They hate orcs more than they dislike the villagers.

**Sylvaris and Rond:** she was hired to make peace with these elves. Let her try. Rond won't stay (book), but give her one improvised line: *"Your Lady kept her word to us. That's more than most humans do. Tell whoever's in charge now."*

> [!tip] Milestone
> Survive the orc siege (with or without the elves) → **level 3**, at the next rest. Book: [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=37|p. 36]].

### Player recap (spoiler-free - copy to the Player Wiki)
In the grey hours before dawn, Shay and Sylvaris kept watch at the gate with two of Lady Nandar's guards, and the last goblin hiding in the village was found and killed. In the keep, Wolfram stood over Lady Nandar's body, found an unfinished letter tucked in her sleeve, and swore a vow. The guards now answer to him.

Shay and Sylvaris did not believe B'Leep's account of his private talk with Kella, and went to question her themselves. Shay saw straight through her: she was no monk. Pressed hard, she admitted she worked with "people who guard caravans", and the party locked her up in the temple. Before they did, a little winged snake slipped away to the south.

At dawn, seven riders came up the trail. The party raised the drawbridge just in time. Their leader, a smiling half-elf named Xolkin, had come for Kella, and the party recognised the riders for what they were: the Zhentarim. After tense talks, a deal was struck. The riders were let in, Kella went back to Xolkin, and the Zhentarim agreed to help hold Nightstone against the orcs coming out of the forest. Afterwards, they would pay five emeralds and ride on.

Then the orcs came: more than twenty of them, fleeing out of the Ardeep Forest, with a huge war chief at their head. The party threw the gate open, hid in the houses, and waited for them to walk into the trap...
