# One-on-one: B'Leep and Kella

> [!info] Before you start
> - **Who:** you and Max, on a Discord voice call. Nobody else listening.
> - **When and where:** 03:00 in-game, upstairs at the Nightstone Inn, in Kella's wrecked room (8f). The others have just left for the keep.
> - **How long:** 10-15 minutes.
> - **Your goal:** Kella reveals she's Zhentarim, tells him friends are coming, and asks him to help her take the village. **B'Leep decides.** There's no wrong answer.
> - Book: Kella [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=26|p. 25]], the Seven Snakes [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=28|p. 27-28]]. Her notes: [[Kella Darkhope]].

## Kella in three lines
- **Voice:** quiet, calm and polite. She answers a question with a question and strokes the winged snake on her left arm while she thinks.
- **Wants:** Nightstone as a base for the Black Network. It has walls and a drawbridge, it's a day's ride from Waterdeep, and now it's empty.
- **Stats if it goes wrong:** Spy (AC 12, 27 HP, Deception +5, Insight +4). She'd rather slip away than fight.

## 1. Set the scene (say it in your own words)
> *The others' footsteps fade down the stairs. Kella waits until the front door closes. Then she sits on the edge of the broken desk, the little winged snake winding around her wrist, and looks at you for a long moment.*
> "So. A gnome with the serpent's mark, walking into my inn in the middle of the night. The road does provide."

## 2. The test
Before she trusts him, she checks he's really "family".
> "Where does the serpent sleep, little cousin?"

- There's no fixed password. **Let Max improvise an answer.** If he answers confidently, she smiles: "Close enough."
- If he fumbles or jokes: "Mm. Every cousin has an off day." She still continues, but she'll watch him more closely.
- If Max asks how B'Leep knows the signs: his background (Faction Agent, Zhentarim) gives him the network's secret signs and passwords.

## 3. The reveal
> "I'm not a monk. You knew that the moment you saw her." *She lifts the snake.* "She's a messenger. She brought me three words a few days ago: 'On our way.'"
> "Friends are coming. Seven riders. The Seven Snakes, they call themselves. They should have been here by now. Dawn at the latest."
> "Look around you, cousin. Walls. A drawbridge. A keep. Nobody left to argue. A day's ride from Waterdeep. The Network has wanted a place like this for years, and the giants just handed it to us."

**If he asks about the leader:** "Xolkin. Charming. Smiles too much. Don't believe half of what he says, and you'll get along fine."
**If he asks who sent them:** "Someone in Daggerford who doesn't like his name said out loud." (That's the Snail; she won't say more.)

## 4. The ask
> "Here's what I need. When the riders come, the drawbridge stays down and your friends stay calm. That paladin especially. Keep his hand off his sword."
> "And if your friends are set on rescuing villagers in some cave? Let them go. Encourage it, even. Empty houses are easier to hold."

**What's in it for him** (B'Leep came for the reward money; he needs parts):
> "Lady Nandar's reward died with her, I'd wager. Have you seen the keep? The Network pays its debts. Gold for your tinkering, and a name with people who matter."

## 5. Likely questions from B'Leep

| He asks | Kella says |
|---|---|
| "What about the villagers?" | "What about them? They ran. If they come back, they'll find new neighbours. Isn't that how every town in the North was built?" (She doesn't know the goblins are holding them prisoner, and doesn't care.) |
| "What happens to my friends?" | "Nothing, if they're sensible. They can rest here, rest well, and ride on. We're not butchers, cousin." |
| "Did you have anything to do with the giants?" | *A short laugh.* "If I could call down castles from the sky, would I be sleeping in a ruined inn?" (True: she didn't.) |
| "The goblin you shot downstairs?" | "It came up the stairs with a knife. I don't lose sleep over goblins." |
| "Why tell me all this?" | "Because you're family, and family looks after family. And because I'd rather have you inside the walls than out." |

**Rolls, if Max asks for them:**
- **Insight** (B'Leep) vs her **Deception +5**: on a win, he senses she means every word about the Network, and that "we're not butchers" is a promise she can't actually make.
- If B'Leep **lies to her** (pretends to agree): his **Deception** vs her **Insight +4**. If she wins, she plays along anyway, but she now considers him a risk.

## 6. How it can end

| B'Leep... | Kella says | What it means for session 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **Agrees** | "Good. Not a word to the others. When they come, you'll know." | He's her inside man. When the riders arrive, the Zhents treat him as an ally and expect him to keep the gate open. |
| **Refuses** | *Cold, still polite:* "Then forget we spoke. And remember who's coming up that road." | She keeps her cover with the others and waits. She may try to lower the drawbridge herself when the riders come. |
| **Plays along but plans to tell the party** | (If she wins the Insight contest, she suspects. Otherwise she believes him.) | Great drama: B'Leep holds the secret, and he chooses when to reveal it. |
| **Says he'll think about it** | "Think fast, cousin. Dawn is coming." | Leave the choice open into session 2. |

**End the scene on a hook:** *Somewhere to the south, very faint, a horse whinnies on the trail. Kella goes still, and smiles.* (The riders are close. Don't bring them in yet; that's the opening of session 2.)
