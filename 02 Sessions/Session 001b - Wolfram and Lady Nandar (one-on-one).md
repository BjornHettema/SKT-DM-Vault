# One-on-one: Wolfram and Lady Nandar

> [!info] Before you start
> - **Who:** you and Wolfram's player, on a Discord voice call.
> - **When and where:** 03:00 in-game, inside Nandar Keep. **Kaelen Zam**, the quiet guard who has been grieving her, leads Wolfram into the half-collapsed great hall (14a).
> - **How long:** 10-15 minutes.
> - **Your goal:** a moment of grief and a vow; a letter that tells Wolfram why he was sent, and that Lady Nandar didn't trust the "monk" at the inn; a flash of his lost memory; and Kaelen asking him to lead.
> - Book: the keep and the guards [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=27|p. 26]]. Notes: [[Lady Velrosa Nandar]], [[Nandar Keep Guards]].

## Kaelen Zam in three lines *(improv)*
- **Look:** young, red-eyed, dust on his tabard, his helmet tucked under his arm.
- **Voice:** quiet, slow, a little hoarse. He talks about her in the present tense, then corrects himself.
- **Wants:** someone to tell him what to do now that she's gone.

## 1. Set the scene (say it in your own words)
> *Kaelen leads you through the dark with a single candle. Half the great hall lies under fallen stone; cold air comes through the broken roof above, and you can see stars through it. On the shattered oak table they've laid out Lady Nandar: a woman of perhaps fifty, in a dusty blue gown, her hands folded. A gold ring with green stones still sits on her finger. Above the hearth hangs a banner: a golden fox with a rose in its teeth.*
> "We dug for her. All four of us. By the time we reached her, she was already gone."

## 2. Who she was (Kaelen talks)
Let him talk while Wolfram looks. Use one or two of these:
> "She paid us out of her own purse. Every tenday, on the dot. Lord Drezlin never did."
> "The elves killed her husband. Shot him on the roof, right up there. And she still made peace with them. Kept her word to them, too. Every day."
> "She asked for help, you know. From Waterdeep. From the temples. Someone to keep us safe." *He looks at Wolfram's armour.* "That was you, wasn't it?"

## 3. The ring
If Wolfram looks at it or reaches for her hands:
> *Kaelen steps closer, very quiet.* "Nobody takes that ring. Not while I'm standing."

If Wolfram respects that, Kaelen trusts him from now on.

## 4. The letter
Tucked into her sleeve, or under her folded hands, is an **unfinished letter**. Kaelen didn't know it was there. You can read it out loud:

> *To the priests of Helm, in Waterdeep.*
> *I thank you for answering. Your promise to send a sworn shield has eased my nights more than you know. Goblins have been seen in the hills to the north, and the elves watch us still, though they keep our peace.*
> *And there is a guest at our inn, a quiet woman named Kella who calls herself a monk. She asks too many questions about our walls, our gate, and how many guards we keep. I have never once seen her pray. I would feel easier once your shield arrives, and*

*The ink trails off.*

- This is **improv**, not from the book. Lady Nandar never got to finish it.
- It confirms Wolfram's hook: **he is the sworn shield she was promised.**
- And it plants doubt about Kella, while B'Leep is alone with her at the inn.
- Wolfram may take the letter. Kaelen won't stop him.

## 5. The flash of memory *(improv)*
When Wolfram looks up through the broken roof at the stars (or when you feel the moment is right), describe it briefly:

> *For a heartbeat the hall is gone. You're on your back in a muddy field. The ground is shaking. Something blots out the sky, a shape far too big to be a cloud. Stone is falling. Someone is shouting your rank, "Corporal! Corporal!", and then everything goes dark.*
> *You're back in the hall. Kaelen is staring at you. "Sir? Are you all right?"*

- No roll, no mechanics. Don't explain it. If the player asks, Wolfram only knows it felt like **a memory, not a dream**.
- DM-only meaning: giants were part of whatever nearly killed him. Keep it vague and consistent later. (Log it in his note under *DM-only: hooks*.)

## 6. Kaelen's worries
> "Who leads us now, sir? Sydiri wants to wait for orders from Waterdeep. Torem wants to run. Alara wants to march north after the villagers, tonight, with four men."
> "The drawbridge was down for three days. Anything could have walked in."
> "And the woman at the inn, the monk. She was here before the giants came. Never saw her pray, either."
> "Will you lead us? Just until someone comes?"

## 7. The vow
Give Wolfram space. A paladin of Helm, the Watcher, the god of guardians, standing over the woman he was sent to protect. If the player makes a vow (to protect the village, to find the villagers, to find who did this), **let it land**: Kaelen kneels too.

**Inspiration:** your table rules say to remember it. A heartfelt vow is a perfect moment: give Wolfram **Inspiration**.

## 8. How it can end

| Wolfram... | Kaelen | What it means for session 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **Takes command** | "Yes, sir." Straightens up for the first time. | The guards follow Wolfram's orders, which matters when the riders and then the orcs come. |
| **Takes the letter** | Doesn't object. | Wolfram knows Lady Nandar distrusted Kella. Will he tell the others, or confront her? |
| **Swears a vow** | Kneels beside him. | Inspiration. Remember the vow: it's a great hook to call back later. |
| **Declines to lead** | "Then what do we do?" | The guards stay leaderless and argue; they'll need someone when trouble comes. |

## 9. After the scene: tell Claude
- [ ] Did he take command of the guards?
- [ ] Did he take the letter, and does he plan to tell anyone about Kella?
- [ ] What did he vow, if anything? Inspiration given?
- [ ] How did the player react to the memory flash?
