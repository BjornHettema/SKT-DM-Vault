# Nightstone - Goblins and Worgs

Session: [[Session 001 - Nightstone]] · Book: [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=21|p. 20-26]] · Map: [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=22|p. 21]]

**How they behave:** the goblins are spread out and looting, so fights come one or two goblins at a time. A goblin that's alone and hurt may run for the temple (5). Once the bell has stopped ringing, goblins near the square (the ones at 4c and 9) come to join a fight there and shoot arrows from the edges.

## Worgs in the square (3)

Two worgs are tearing at a dead dog in the northeast corner of the square. They attack anyone who enters the square and fight to the death.

> [!warning] Level 1 danger
> A bite does 2d6+3 (avg 10), enough to drop B'Leep, Shay or Sylvaris in one hit, and it can knock a target prone. Describe them clearly first so the players can plan. Options: fight from the edge of the square, use the buildings, or lure the worgs one at a time.
