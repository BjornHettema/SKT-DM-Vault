# Nightstone - Ear Seekers

Session: [[Session 002 - Riders at Dawn]] · Book: [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=29|p. 28]] · Map: [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=22|p. 21]]

**Who:** war chief **Gurrash** (Orc War Chief, wounded: 60 of 93 HP), **Norgra One-Eye** (Orc Eye of Gruumsh, his lieutenant; takes command if Gurrash dies) and **20 orcs**. They wear necklaces of elf ears. Beaten by the Ardeep wood elves, they're fleeing south with the elves not far behind, and want to make a stand in Nightstone. No gear except weapons and armour.

## How it plays out
1. **Drawbridge down:** they rush it and storm the village, killing anything in their way.
2. **Drawbridge up:** they swim the moat and try to climb the palisade, and fail. Gurrash sends scouts downriver.
3. **After 10 minutes** they find the gap in the palisade where the broken bridge (11) leads up to the keep, and come in there.
4. **Inside:** they fight to the death and take no prisoners.
5. The party can **hide and pick off a few at a time**, or **retreat to the keep**, which the orcs ignore.
6. **Morale:** if they lose **more than half** (11+) without gaining a foothold in the village, the rest flee into the hills.
7. **Strange Bedfellows:** if the party came to terms with the Zhents, Xolkin and Kella help: they raise the drawbridge, man the watchtowers and shoot. They keep their distance from orc axes.
8. **Elves to the rescue:** if the party is in danger of being overwhelmed, **8 wood elf scouts** led by **Rond Arrowhome** arrive from the north. Afterwards: *"You're welcome!"* and they leave. No reward, no peace talks.

Survive → **level 3** (at the next rest).

## Running 22 orcs as a new DM
- **Squads, not individuals.** Split the 20 orcs into 4 squads of 5. Each squad gets **one initiative** and moves together. Gurrash and Norgra each have their own.
- **They come in waves.** Only the orcs that can reach the party matter. At the gap, only one or two can come through at a time.
- **Aggressive:** each orc can use a bonus action to move up to its speed toward an enemy it can see. They close distance fast.
- **Gurrash's Battle Cry** (once per day): every orc of his choice within 30 ft gets advantage on attacks until his next turn, and he gets one bonus attack. Save it for the moment they break through.
- **Count the dead.** At 11 dead without a foothold, they flee.
- **Describe numbers, not hit points:** "the orc staggers, blood on its tusks" is better than "it's at 4 HP".
- **Shay speaks Orc.** Let him hear Gurrash's orders (*"Downriver! Find another way in!"*). It gives the party a chance to get to the gap first.
