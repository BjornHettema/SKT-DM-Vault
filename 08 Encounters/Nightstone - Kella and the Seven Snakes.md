# Nightstone - Kella and the Seven Snakes

Session: [[Session 001 - Nightstone]] (Kella) · next session (Seven Snakes) · Book: [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=26|Kella p. 25]], [[Storm Kings Thunder.pdf#page=28|Seven Snakes p. 27-28]]

## Kella Darkhope (tonight)

- Hides in her wrecked room above the inn's dining room and listens. Spot her: **Perception vs her Stealth**.
- **If found:** she plays a guest who was knocked out by debris and left behind, and claims the goblins trapped her. See through it: **Insight vs her Deception**. The snake on her arm is "just a pet". Any Zhentarim member, so **B'Leep**, knows it's a Zhent messenger snake.
- **Her goal:** turn Nightstone into a Zhentarim base. Her friends are already on the way.
- **With B'Leep:** she'll quietly test whether he's "family" and try to recruit him. This is a dilemma for him, not a trap. Do it as a private message if you like (DM-to-player messages are allowed).
- She carries 8 gp and 5 sp.

## The Seven Snakes (next session, after the goblins are cleared and before a long rest)

- Seven Zhentarim riders led by **[[Xolkin Alassandar]]**. He's charming and ruthless, and in love with Kella, though she doesn't feel the same.
- They ride in and call for Kella. If the drawbridge is up, they ask for it to be lowered.
- **What they want:** to take over the village. They'll **pay for Kella's freedom** if she's a prisoner (five 100 gp gems), and try force if that fails.
- **What the players can do:** fight, bargain, let them have the village, or trick them. The book doesn't require a fight. Ending this peacefully is a valid outcome.
- In a fight, Xolkin first drinks his *potion of invulnerability*. He also carries 4 pp, 13 gp and a Zhent signet ring (25 gp), and each rider has 2d10 gp.
- **Milestone:** surviving the Snakes and the orc siege that follows takes the party to **level 3**.

> [!warning] Session 2 prep: the orc siege ("Ear Seekers", p. 28)
> After the Snakes, a war chief (already down to 60 HP), an Eye of Gruumsh and **20 orcs** try to storm the village. In a straight fight that's far beyond a level-2 party. The book expects the players to raise the drawbridge, hide, pick off orcs, or retreat to the keep, which the orcs ignore. If things go badly, eight elf scouts arrive to help. Prep this one carefully before session 2.
